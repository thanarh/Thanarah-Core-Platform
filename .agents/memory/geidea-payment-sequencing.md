The user had already required bank transfer with receipt and admin approval for both billing flows before provider integration, with card and Apple Pay inactive and marked “coming soon.”

**How to apply:** Do not connect or implement Paymob before the bank-transfer workflows for both billing flows are complete and the rest of the roadmap is ready. At the final stage, confirm Paymob test-environment access and approved business rules; keep production payments off until explicitly approved.
