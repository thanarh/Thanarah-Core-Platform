# Phase 35B — Resolve MongoDB Deployment Decision

Perform a documentation-only decision-preparation phase for Thanarah's tenant MongoDB deployment.

## Objective

Resolve the missing deployment assumptions identified in Phase 35A without inventing infrastructure facts or changing application behavior.

## Inspect

Review:
- `TENANT_MONGODB_DEPLOYMENT_DECISION.md`
- `README.md`
- `replit.md`
- Current tenant storage registry and activation implementation
- The three patient duplicate-prevention index definitions

## Required output

Prepare a concise decision record with these fields:

1. **Provider:** Confirmed or PENDING.
2. **Supported MongoDB server version:** Confirmed or PENDING.
3. **Deployment topology:** Standalone, replica set, or sharded — only if supported by explicit evidence; otherwise PENDING.
4. **Patient collection sharding policy:** Sharding prohibited, permitted, or PENDING.
5. **Shard key:** Record only if an actual sharded deployment has been approved; otherwise NOT APPLICABLE or PENDING.
6. **Duplicate enforcement:** Explain whether the current three unique indexes establish the required guarantee under the confirmed topology.
7. **Non-production validation:** Identify the server version and topology required to match the approved deployment.
8. **Outstanding approval:** Identify the infrastructure owner or decision-maker needed to resolve each unknown.

Separate repository-confirmed facts from proposed decisions and external assumptions.

If no authoritative infrastructure decision is available, retain `PENDING APPROVAL`. Do not select Atlas Free, a provider, a server version, or a topology on the user's behalf.

## Restrictions

- Documentation and decision preparation only.
- Do not edit application code, indexes, schemas, tests, or deployment configuration.
- Do not run tests or migrations.
- Do not connect to production or inspect credentials.
- Do not stage or commit.
- Preserve the existing deployment decision document unless a clearly separated proposed decision record is necessary.
- Do not claim production readiness.

Stop after reporting the proposed decision record and the remaining approvals required.
