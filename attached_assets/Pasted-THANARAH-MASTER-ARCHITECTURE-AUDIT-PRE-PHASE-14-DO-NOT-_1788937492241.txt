==================================================
A. THANARAH PRODUCT IDENTITY
==================================================

Thanarah (ثناره) is intended to become a production-grade, multi-tenant Healthcare SaaS platform.

It is NOT:
- a prototype
- a demo
- a single-clinic application
- a collection of unrelated CRUD modules
- an AI-first product
- a frontend-only system

The architecture must support long-term commercial operation.

Code, naming, structure, APIs, comments, metadata, and project organization must remain professional and human-engineered.

Do not introduce AI-generated-looking architecture, unnecessary abstractions, fake placeholder systems, or temporary shortcuts.

==================================================
B. CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The system must separate:

Platform
Tenant / Organization
Clinic / Facility
Branch
Staff
Doctor
Patient
Authentication
Authorization
Subscriptions
Licensing
Healthcare data

Do not collapse these concepts into one User model or one global role system.

Platform identity and tenant identity must remain conceptually separate.

==================================================
C. PLATFORM LAYER
==================================================

The current platform layer should support:

- Platform users
- Platform roles
- Platform permissions
- Platform authentication
- Platform sessions
- Refresh-token rotation
- Security events
- Audit logging
- Platform administration
- Platform configuration
- System health

The completed authentication/authorization architecture must remain intact.

Verify that:

PlatformAuthGuard
and
PlatformAuthorizationGuard

remain separate responsibilities.

Authentication:
"Who are you?"

Authorization:
"What are you allowed to do?"

Do not merge them.

==================================================
D. AUTHENTICATION FOUNDATION
==================================================

Verify the completed authentication architecture:

- PlatformUser
- PlatformAuthGuard
- AuthenticatedPlatformUser
- @CurrentPlatformUser()
- @Public()
- AccessTokenService
- JWT validation
- PlatformSession
- RefreshTokenService
- RefreshTokenHistory
- AuthCookieService
- session revocation
- logout
- logout-all

Verify:

- JWT remains minimal.
- Refresh tokens are opaque.
- Raw refresh tokens are never persisted.
- Refresh token hashes are persisted.
- Token rotation is atomic.
- Token reuse detection exists.
- Session ownership is validated.
- tokenVersion behavior is preserved.
- session history is preserved.

Do not redesign this architecture.

==================================================
E. AUTHORIZATION FOUNDATION
==================================================

Verify the completed platform authorization architecture:

PlatformAuthGuard
        ↓
PlatformAuthorizationGuard
        ↓
Controller

Verify:

- permissions are resolved server-side.
- permissions are not trusted from clients.
- permissions are not stored as a full list inside JWT.
- platform roles and tenant roles are conceptually separate.
- protected platform roles remain protected.
- OWNER assignment restrictions remain enforced.
- 401 and 403 remain distinct.

Do not introduce tenant authorization into the platform authorization guard.

Tenant authorization must eventually be a separate layer.

F. MULTI-TENANCY
==================================================

This is the most important upcoming architectural boundary.

Determine whether the current architecture can safely support:

Tenant / Organization
    ↓
Clinics / Facilities
    ↓
Branches
    ↓
Staff
    ↓
Doctors
    ↓
Patients
    ↓
Healthcare records

Every tenant-owned resource must eventually have an unambiguous tenant boundary.

Audit for:

- missing tenant identifiers
- unsafe global queries
- repositories that cannot become tenant-scoped
- services that assume one organization
- controllers that assume one organization
- global IDs that could cause cross-tenant access
- future authorization problems

Do NOT add tenant fields yet.

Only identify what must be designed correctly before implementation.

==================================================

The future SaaS layer should support organizations/tenants as first-class entities.

A tenant should be able to represent a healthcare business such as:

- clinic

The architecture should support:

Tenant

multiple facilities/clinics

Do not hard-code Thanarah around "one clinic per tenant".

==================================================

Future architecture must allow:

Platform identity

    ↓
Facility/branch scope

Audit whether current identity architecture can support a user belonging to one or more tenants without redesigning PlatformUser.

Do not implement membership yet.
