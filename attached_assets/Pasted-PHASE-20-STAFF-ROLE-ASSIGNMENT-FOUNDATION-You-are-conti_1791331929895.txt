PHASE 20 — STAFF ROLE ASSIGNMENT FOUNDATION

You are continuing the Thanarah production SaaS architecture.

This is a REAL commercial multi-tenant healthcare SaaS system.
Do NOT treat this as a prototype.
Do NOT invent AI-looking abstractions, placeholder architecture, unnecessary abstractions, fake production behavior, or speculative future implementations.

IMPORTANT:
Implement ONLY Phase 20.
Do not start Phase 21.
Do not implement doctors, patients, departments, specialties, appointments, scheduling, medical records, prescriptions, billing, insurance, MongoDB healthcare data, notifications, or Voice AI.

==================================================
1. CURRENT ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Thanarah uses:

CENTRAL DATABASE
- Prisma 6
- MySQL/MariaDB

TENANT DATABASES
- MongoDB
- Future healthcare/operational data only

Existing identity hierarchy:

Platform User
    ↓
Tenant Membership
    ↓
Tenant Staff
    ↓
Tenant Role Assignment
    ↓
Tenant Permissions
    ↓
Future Facility / Branch scope
    ↓
Business operations

Important distinctions:

Platform User != Tenant Membership
Tenant Membership != Staff
Staff != Role
Role != Permission
Permission != Entitlement
Entitlement != Quota

Preserve these distinctions.

==================================================
2. PHASE 20 GOAL
==================================================

Build the foundation for assigning existing tenant roles to existing staff members.

The purpose of this phase is:

Staff
    ↓
Staff Role Assignment
    ↓
Existing Tenant Role
    ↓
Existing Tenant Permissions

The role assignment is an authorization relationship.

Do NOT copy permissions into Staff.

Do NOT store role names directly on Staff.

Do NOT create a new role system.

Do NOT modify the platform role system.

Reuse the existing TenantRole / TenantPermission architecture already implemented in previous phases.

==================================================
4. CORE RELATIONSHIP
==================================================

The intended conceptual model is:

Tenant
 ├── TenantMembership
 │     └── PlatformUser
 │
 ├── Staff
 │     └── TenantMembership
 │
 └── TenantRole
       └── TenantPermission

StaffRoleAssignment connects:

Staff
    +
TenantRole

Both must belong to the SAME tenant.

This relationship must be enforced by application logic AND database constraints where practical.

Never rely only on a client-supplied tenantId.

TrustedTenantContext is authoritative.

==================================================
5. ROLE ASSIGNMENT MODEL
==================================================

If a new StaffRoleAssignment model is necessary, use the project's established ID/timestamp conventions.

Conceptually:

StaffRoleAssignment
- id
- tenantId only if required for safe relational integrity / established architecture
- staffId
- roleId
- createdAt
- updatedAt if appropriate

Do not duplicate tenant ownership unnecessarily.

If tenantId is included, enforce:

Staff.tenantId == StaffRoleAssignment.tenantId
Role.tenantId == StaffRoleAssignment.tenantId

Do not allow drift.

Recommended uniqueness:

(staffId, roleId)

A staff member should not receive the exact same role twice.

Do NOT implement role priority or inheritance in this phase.

==================================================
6. ROLE ASSIGNMENT SEMANTICS
==================================================

Role assignment means:

"This staff member has this tenant role."

It does NOT mean:

- creating a new role
- changing the role definition
- copying permissions
- creating platform permissions
- granting subscription entitlement
- bypassing tenant membership
- granting facility/branch access
- granting healthcare privileges

The role's permissions remain authoritative in the existing TenantRole → TenantPermission system.

==================================================
7. MEMBERSHIP INTEGRITY
==================================================

A Staff record already belongs to an existing TenantMembership.

Role assignment must remain consistent with that relationship.

Before assignment verify:

1. TrustedTenantContext is valid
2. staff exists
3. staff belongs to current tenant
4. staff's membership belongs to current tenant
5. staff's membership is the expected membership for that staff record
6. membership is still eligible according to existing lifecycle rules
7. role exists
8. role belongs to current tenant
9. role is eligible for assignment according to existing role lifecycle rules
10. no duplicate assignment exists

Do not allow a StaffRoleAssignment to become a bypass around TenantMembership.

==================================================
8. STAFF / ROLE LIFECYCLE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Follow the existing lifecycle conventions.

Important:

If Staff is:
- SUSPENDED
- DEACTIVATED

do not silently treat the staff member as fully authorized.

The existing authorization resolution must remain authoritative.

Do not invent a second authorization engine.

If the current tenant authorization architecture resolves permissions from TenantMembership rather than Staff role assignments, extend it carefully so the new Staff role relationship is incorporated without breaking existing membership authorization.

The final effective authorization must remain server-side.

10. AUTHORIZATION
==================================================

Use the existing tenant authorization architecture.

Introduce only the minimum permissions required if they do not already exist.

Suggested:

First inspect the existing permission naming convention.

Recommended semantics:

- view staff role assignments

MANAGE:
- assign role

Do not allow role definition editing from staff role endpoints.

Role management remains a separate concern.

==================================================
12. ROLE ASSIGNMENT
==================================================

When assigning:

Validate:

- authenticated actor
- TrustedTenantContext
- staff tenant ownership
- staff membership integrity
- role tenant ownership
- role eligibility
- actor has staff-role management permission
- no duplicate assignment

Then persist.

Return a safe representation.

Do not expose internal Prisma structures directly.

==================================================
13. ROLE REMOVAL
==================================================

Role removal must:

- verify staff belongs to current tenant
- verify role belongs to current tenant
- verify assignment exists
- verify actor has management permission
- remove only that assignment

Do not delete the Staff record.

Do not deactivate the membership.

Do not modify the TenantRole itself.

Do not modify TenantPermission records.

Do not modify subscription or entitlement state.

==================================================
14. AUTHORIZATION RESOLUTION
==================================================

This is the most important architectural requirement.

Inspect how TenantAuthorizationGuard currently determines permissions.

Extend it only if necessary so that:

Staff
→ assigned TenantRole(s)
→ TenantPermission(s)

can participate correctly in authorization.

Do NOT create:

StaffAuthorizationGuard
StaffPermissionService
StaffAuthEngine

if the existing tenant authorization infrastructure can be extended cleanly.

The system should have ONE tenant authorization model.

Avoid duplicate permission-resolution paths.

The server must remain authoritative.

Never trust:
- role IDs from JWT
- permissions from JWT
- request body roles
- query-string permissions
- custom headers
- cookies

==================================================
15. IMPORTANT AUTHORIZATION QUESTION
==================================================

Determine how current TenantMembership roles and Staff roles interact.

Do NOT silently replace existing membership authorization.

Choose the smallest architecture-consistent behavior.

If the existing system already treats TenantMembership as the authorization subject, determine whether Staff role assignments should:

A) become the authorization roles for the membership, or
B) be an additional server-resolved role source.

Do not guess.

Inspect the current implementation and document the decision.

The resulting authorization behavior must be deterministic.

Avoid accidental privilege multiplication.

Do NOT implement role inheritance or automatic privilege escalation.

==================================================
17. SECURITY TESTS
==================================================

Must test:

Authentication:
- unauthenticated request → 401

Authorization:
- missing staff-role read permission → denied
- missing staff-role manage permission → denied
- client-supplied permissions ignored
- client-supplied tenantId ignored

Tenant isolation:
- staff from tenant A cannot receive role from tenant B
- role from tenant B cannot be assigned to staff from tenant A
- staff role listing cannot cross tenants
- role removal cannot cross tenants

Integrity:
- duplicate assignment rejected or idempotently handled according to project conventions
- invalid staff rejected
- invalid role rejected
- mismatched membership rejected
- invalid/deactivated staff handled correctly
- invalid role lifecycle handled correctly

Privilege:
- assigning a role must produce exactly the permissions defined by that role
- role assignment must not modify role definition
- role assignment must not modify permission definitions
- staff cannot escalate to platform roles
- staff role assignment cannot bypass TenantMembership
