## Permanent architecture constraints

- Thanarah is a multi-tenant healthcare SaaS platform.
- Central platform/control-plane data remains separate from tenant healthcare records.
- Tenant Patient records belong in tenant-isolated MongoDB storage.
- Production database credentials must remain in the production environment and must never be printed or exposed.
- Replit development must not connect to production databases.
- One logical tenant may eventually use multiple physical MongoDB databases, but do not implement cluster rotation, sharding, automatic rebalancing, or archive routing without an approved design.
- Patient duplicate prevention must remain tenant-scoped.
- The current deployment decision must remain `PENDING APPROVAL` until authoritative evidence exists.
